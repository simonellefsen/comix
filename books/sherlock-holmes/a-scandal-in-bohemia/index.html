A Scandal in BohemiaBaker Street

Undated, unsigned

There will call upon you to-night, at a quarter to eight o’clock, a gentleman who desires to consult you upon a matter of the very deepest moment. Your recent services to one of the royal houses of Europe have shown that you are one who may safely be trusted.

Be in your chamber then at that hour, and do not take it amiss if your visitor wear a mask.

To be left till called for

My dear Mr Sherlock Holmes,—You really did it very well. You took me in completely. Until after the alarm of fire, I had not a suspicion.

I have been trained as an actress myself. Male costume is nothing new to me. I wished you good-night, rather imprudently.

As to the photograph, your client may rest in peace. I love and am loved by a better man than he. I keep it only to safeguard myself.

Irene Norton, née Adler
